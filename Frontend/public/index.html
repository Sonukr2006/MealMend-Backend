How It Works

Step 1: Collection

We collect food waste from your home or business.

Step 2: Sorting

Sorting food items for proper recycling or composting.

Step 3: Recycling

Turning food waste into useful compost or resources.

Step 4: Distribution

Delivering compost to community gardens and farmers.

For High-Quality Food Donations

Step 1: Food Donation

Donors provide details about the high-quality food they wish to donate, including type, quantity, and quality.

Step 2: Quality Assessment

Our team assesses the donated food to ensure it meets safety and quality standards.

Step 3: Collection

We arrange for the collection of the food from the donor's location at a convenient time.

Step 4: Distribution

The collected food is delivered to local charities, shelters, or community organizations that can utilize it effectively.
